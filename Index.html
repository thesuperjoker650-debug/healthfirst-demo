Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book.

Do I need to fast before my test?

Lipid profile and glucose tests usually need 8–10 hours of fasting. CBC, thyroid, HbA1c and Vitamin D do not. We will tell you what to do when you book.

Is home collection really free?

Yes. Home collection is free in our service areas. Message us your pincode to confirm.

How will I receive my report?

You will get a PDF on WhatsApp and email, usually within 24 hours. Printed copies are available at our centre.

Can I get tested without a doctor’s prescription?

Yes. Most health checkups and routine tests can be booked without a prescription. Please show your report to a doctor for advice.

How do I pay?

You can pay by UPI, card or cash when the sample is collected, or online while booking.
